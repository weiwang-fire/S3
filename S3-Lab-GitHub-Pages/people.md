## Dr Wei Wang

**Founder & Group Lead · Lecturer in Fire Safety Engineering, RMIT University**

Wei founded S³ Lab and established a battery safety laboratory at RMIT. He guides the group's scientific direction, experimental planning and researcher development. His work connects materials science, thermal management and fire safety engineering.

[RMIT profile](https://www.rmit.edu.au/profiles/w/wei-wang) · [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=RQ-WOToAAAAJ) · [ORCID](https://orcid.org/0000-0001-5788-6314)

## Research team and supervision

The team brings together postdoctoral researchers and PhD candidates working across battery safety, fire protection and materials engineering.

At RMIT, Wei provides primary day-to-day supervision for four PhD candidates as a joint senior supervisor and mentors two postdoctoral researchers. He also supervises two PhD candidates at UNSW Sydney.

Research training covers experimental planning, materials characterisation, interpretation of thermal data and scientific communication. Researchers contribute their own questions and develop increasing responsibility for their work.

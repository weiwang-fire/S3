## Passive fire protection for residential battery energy storage

**Supported by the Building and Plumbing Commission**

This research investigates passive protection for residential battery energy storage installations. It brings together battery materials and fire safety expertise to examine thermal exposure and protective performance.

**Wei's role.** Wei serves as a senior supervisor, guiding research and experimental planning with the project researchers.

## Fire safety education for the energy transition

**Victorian Government Skills Solutions Partnerships Round 3**

Wei contributes as a Chief Investigator and coordinator to an RMIT project developing microcredentials on emerging fire risks in the built environment. He is responsible for leading one of the two planned microcredentials.

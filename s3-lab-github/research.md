## Battery and energy storage safety

We investigate how battery materials and systems behave under thermal stress, and how protective materials can contribute to safer energy storage. Our work includes separator materials and passive fire protection for residential battery installations.

Published separator research provides a foundation in thermal stability, porous structures and ion transport. Current residential battery protection research examines a different application, where external protection must address heat transfer to the surrounding built environment.

## Fire-resistant materials and interfaces

We study protective coatings, polymer nanocomposites and porous materials. We are interested in how nanoscale organisation and interfaces influence thermal stability, char formation, heat transfer and structural integrity during heating.

Published work includes MXene-based membranes and frameworks, layered coatings, nanoclay-containing composites and flame-retardant polymer systems.

## Thermal management and building materials

We explore materials that address heat transfer and fire safety together. Research includes porous insulation and wood-derived composites for daytime radiative cooling and fire resistance.

The relationship between performance under normal conditions and response to severe thermal exposure is a central question across these applications.

## How we work

We combine material preparation, characterisation and thermal testing with modelling where appropriate. We use experimental observations to examine proposed mechanisms and distinguish measured performance from model predictions.

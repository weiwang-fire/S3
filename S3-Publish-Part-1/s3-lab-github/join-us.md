## PhD Opportunities

Interested in doctoral research on safe, smart and sustainable materials and systems? Our research spans battery safety, thermal management, fire-retardant composites, protective structures, and functional textiles and wearable sensors.

To enquire about supervision and potential opportunities, please send Dr Wei Wang your CV, academic background and a short description of your research interests.

## Postdoctoral Opportunities

Researchers interested in working with S³ Lab are welcome to enquire about potential postdoctoral opportunities and fellowship collaborations. Please include your CV, selected publications and a brief outline of how your expertise connects with our research.

## Get in touch

Email [Dr Wei Wang](mailto:wei.wang@rmit.edu.au) to discuss potential opportunities. Specific positions, funding arrangements and application requirements will be listed here when available.
